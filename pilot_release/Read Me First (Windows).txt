HYLIGHT PILOT FOR WINDOWS  -  v0.7.0                    Danko Panko's HyLight
==========================================================================

HyLight turns your HyLight button into a glucose light. It reads your Dexcom
numbers (or your Nightscout site, or xDrip+) and glows a color when
something needs your attention.

IMPORTANT: This is a pilot of a convenience light. It is NOT a medical device.
Keep using your CGM app and its alarms exactly as you do today.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
 - A PC running Windows 10 or 11, and your HyLight button
 - A Dexcom CGM with Dexcom Share turned on (Dexcom app > Settings > Share,
   with at least one follower added)
 - The Dexcom login of the person WEARING the sensor. Do not use a
   follower's account - it will log in but show no readings.
 OR, instead of Dexcom Share: a Nightscout site, or xDrip+ on an Android
   phone on the same Wi-Fi. See "USING NIGHTSCOUT OR xDRIP+" below.


SET UP (about 5 minutes)
------------------------
1. Right-click the zip and choose "Extract All...", then Extract. Don't run
   HyLight from inside the zip - it won't work from there.
   Move the extracted "HyLight" folder somewhere it can stay, for example
   your Documents folder. Keep everything in that folder together.

2. Plug in the button.

3. Open the HyLight folder and double-click HyLight.exe. The first time,
   Windows will say "Windows protected your PC", because HyLight isn't
   signed by a big publisher yet. This is expected. To open it:
     click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   If your antivirus blocks or removes HyLight, that is a false alarm
   common to new apps; restore it from your antivirus's quarantine or
   allow it, then extract the zip again.

4. Your browser opens a short setup tour. On the first screen, choose where
   your glucose comes from:
     Dexcom: sign in with the Dexcom login of the person wearing the
        sensor, and pick your region.
     Nightscout or xDrip+: see the next section.
   Then click "Connect and continue". Five quick screens then explain the
   lights and choose your hours, high levels, ramp sensitivity and
   on-demand length. (You can take the tour again any time from the
   settings page.)
   No account yet? Click "Connect later" to look around first. (You can
   also start the tour from the connect page if you haven't connected yet.)
   Your login, token or secret is stored safely in Windows Credential
   Manager.

5. The status line at the top of the page should say
   "Button connected - showing ... BG ...". You're set.

TIP: HyLight runs in the background with a small red sun icon next to the
clock (it may be hidden under the ^ arrow - you can drag it out onto the
taskbar). Click the sun to bring the settings page back, or double-click
HyLight.exe again. The page lives at http://127.0.0.1:8765 if you'd like a
bookmark.

TIP: For a desktop shortcut, right-click HyLight.exe > Send to > Desktop
(create shortcut). On Windows 11, click "Show more options" first.


USING NIGHTSCOUT OR xDRIP+
--------------------------
Choose the "Nightscout or xDrip+" tab on the first setup screen (or on the
connect page).

 Nightscout - works anywhere: home, school, office.
   Address: your Nightscout site, e.g. https://your-site.example.com
   Token:   make a read-only one. In Nightscout: Admin Tools > Add new
            Subject, give it a name and the role "readable", save, then
            copy its access token (it looks like school-a595d5f0aeff64c0).
            Your API secret also works, but it can change data too, so a
            token is safer - and deleting the token later cuts HyLight off.
   Anything that uploads to Nightscout works: xDrip+, xDrip4iOS, Juggluco,
   AAPS, Loop, Trio and others, with Dexcom or Libre sensors.

 xDrip+ on the same Wi-Fi - Android only, no Nightscout needed.
   In xDrip+: Settings > Inter-app settings > turn on "xDrip Web Service"
   and "Open Web Service". Setting an "xDrip Web Service Secret" there is
   a good idea.
   Address: http://PHONE-IP:17580, where PHONE-IP is the phone's Wi-Fi IP
            address (in Android's Wi-Fi settings, open the connected
            network's details).
   Secret:  the xDrip Web Service Secret. Leave it blank if none is set.
   The phone and this PC must be on the same Wi-Fi, and the phone's
   address can change. School and office Wi-Fi often stop phones and
   computers from talking to each other; Nightscout avoids all of that.

HyLight checks the address before saving it, and says what's wrong if it
can't connect.


USING THE BUTTON
----------------
 Colors   GREEN  = in range (only shown when you tap the button)
          WHITE  = glucose is ramping up
          YELLOW = high: above your yellow threshold
          RED    = very high: above your red threshold
          PURPLE = low: below 70
          BLUE   = two quick flashes: an on-demand hour started or ended
 Tap          Shows your current status for 3 seconds, any time of day.
              Stays dark if HyLight has no reading from the last 10 minutes.
 Press & hold Mutes the current alert for 30 minutes. Hold again to un-mute.
 Double tap   Turns the button on for an hour, even outside your active
              window (handy for an evening work session). Two blue flashes
              confirm it; two more blue flashes mean the hour is up.
              Double tap again to restart the hour.

Automatic lights only happen inside your "active window" (default 11:30 AM -
8:00 PM) or an on-demand hour. Change the window, thresholds, ramp sensitivity
(or turn ramp detection off) and on-demand length on the settings page.

A light goes out once its reading is over 10 minutes old, even if HyLight
can't reach your glucose source to get a newer one.


QUITTING AND RESTARTING
-----------------------
Quit with "Quit HyLight" at the bottom of the settings page, or right-click
the sun icon by the clock and choose "Quit HyLight". Either turns the light
off. The goodbye page after "Quit HyLight" has a "Restart HyLight instead"
button if you meant to restart it.
Shutting down or signing out of Windows also turns the light off.
If HyLight is ever ended from Task Manager, the button can stay lit. Just
open HyLight again and quit it normally.
To stop using HyLight: click the "Disconnect" button under "Glucose source"
on the settings page, quit, then delete the HyLight folder.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
 "Button not detected": unplug and replug the button. Only one program can
    use the button at a time, so close anything else that might be using it.
 No light when you expect one: look at the status line at the top of the
    page. It shows the BG HyLight sees and what it is showing, or why it has
    no BG. Remember automatic lights only appear inside the active window.
 No BG number at all (Dexcom): Share is probably off, or you signed in with
    a follower's account. Disconnect, then sign in again with the wearer's.
 "no reading in the last 10 minutes" (Nightscout or xDrip+): readings
    stopped arriving. Check the phone is on, xDrip+ (or your uploader) is
    running, and - for Nightscout - that it's still uploading.
 "Couldn't reach ...": check the address. For xDrip+, check the phone is
    on the same Wi-Fi with the web service on, and its IP hasn't changed.


FEEDBACK
--------
Please tell me about anything confusing, wrong, or annoying - including
whether the colors and timing feel right in real life. If something breaks,
send me this log file. To find it, press Windows key + R, paste this, and
press Enter:
    %USERPROFILE%\.dexcom_led_button
The file is hylight.log. It records your glucose readings and your Dexcom
username or Nightscout / xDrip+ address, but never your password, token or
secret. Share it only if you are comfortable doing so.

Privacy: HyLight only talks to the glucose source you choose (Dexcom's
servers, your Nightscout site, or the xDrip+ phone) and to your own PC.
Your login, token or secret is kept in Windows Credential Manager.
