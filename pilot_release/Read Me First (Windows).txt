HYLIGHT PILOT FOR WINDOWS  -  v0.4.0                    Danko Panko's HyLight
==========================================================================

HyLight turns your HyLight button into a glucose light. It reads your Dexcom
numbers and glows a color when something needs your attention.

IMPORTANT: This is a pilot of a convenience light. It is NOT a medical device.
Keep using your Dexcom app and its alarms exactly as you do today.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
 - A PC running Windows 10 or 11, and your HyLight button
 - A Dexcom CGM with Dexcom Share turned on (Dexcom app > Settings > Share,
   with at least one follower added)
 - The Dexcom login of the person WEARING the sensor. Do not use a
   follower's account - it will log in but show no readings.


SET UP (about 5 minutes)
------------------------
1. Right-click the zip and choose "Extract All...", then Extract. Don't run
   HyLight from inside the zip - it won't work from there.
   Move the extracted "HyLight" folder somewhere it can stay, for example
   your Documents folder. Keep everything in that folder together.

2. Plug in the button.

3. Open the HyLight folder and double-click HyLight.exe. The first time,
   Windows will say "Windows protected your PC", because HyLight isn't
   signed by a big publisher yet. This is expected. To open it:
     click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   If your antivirus blocks or removes HyLight, that is a false alarm
   common to new apps; restore it from your antivirus's quarantine or
   allow it, then extract the zip again.

4. Your browser opens HyLight's settings page. Sign in with the Dexcom login
   of the person wearing the sensor, pick your region, and click Connect.
   Your login is stored safely in Windows Credential Manager.

5. The status line at the top of the page should say
   "Button connected - showing ... BG ...". You're set.

TIP: HyLight runs in the background with a small red sun icon next to the
clock (it may be hidden under the ^ arrow - you can drag it out onto the
taskbar). Click the sun to bring the settings page back, or double-click
HyLight.exe again. The page lives at http://127.0.0.1:8765 if you'd like a
bookmark.

TIP: For a desktop shortcut, right-click HyLight.exe > Send to > Desktop
(create shortcut). On Windows 11, click "Show more options" first.


USING THE BUTTON
----------------
 Colors   GREEN  = in range (only shown when you tap the button)
          WHITE  = glucose is ramping up
          YELLOW = high: above your yellow threshold
          RED    = very high: above your red threshold
          PURPLE = low: below 70
          BLUE   = your reminder timer finished
 Tap          Shows your current status for 3 seconds, any time of day.
 Press & hold Mutes the current alert for a while. Hold again to un-mute.
 Double tap   Starts a reminder timer (default 20 minutes). The button
              flashes blue to confirm, then glows blue when time is up.

Automatic lights only happen inside your "active window" (default 11:30 AM -
8:00 PM). Change the window, thresholds, mute length and reminder time on
the settings page.


QUITTING AND RESTARTING
-----------------------
Quit with "Quit HyLight" at the bottom of the settings page, or right-click
the sun icon by the clock and choose "Quit HyLight". Either turns the light
off. The goodbye page after "Quit HyLight" has a "Restart HyLight instead"
button if you meant to restart it.
Shutting down or signing out of Windows also turns the light off.
If HyLight is ever ended from Task Manager, the button can stay lit. Just
open HyLight again and quit it normally.
To stop using HyLight: click "Disconnect Dexcom account" on the settings
page, quit, then delete the HyLight folder.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
 "Button not detected": unplug and replug the button. Only one program can
    use the button at a time, so close anything else that might be using it.
 No light when you expect one: look at the status line at the top of the
    page. It shows the BG HyLight sees and what it is showing. Remember
    automatic lights only appear inside the active window.
 No BG number at all: Share is probably off, or you signed in with a
    follower's account. Disconnect, then sign in again with the wearer's.


FEEDBACK
--------
Please tell me about anything confusing, wrong, or annoying - including
whether the colors and timing feel right in real life. If something breaks,
send me this log file. To find it, press Windows key + R, paste this, and
press Enter:
    %USERPROFILE%\.dexcom_led_button
The file is hylight.log. It records your glucose readings and Dexcom
username, but never your password. Share it only if you are comfortable
doing so.

Privacy: HyLight only talks to Dexcom's servers and to your own PC. Your
Dexcom login is kept in Windows Credential Manager.
