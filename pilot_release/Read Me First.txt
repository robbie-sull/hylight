HYLIGHT PILOT  -  v0.4.0                                  Danko Panko's HyLight
==========================================================================

HyLight turns your HyLight button into a glucose light. It reads your Dexcom
numbers and glows a color when something needs your attention.

IMPORTANT: This is a pilot of a convenience light. It is NOT a medical device.
Keep using your Dexcom app and its alarms exactly as you do today.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
 - A Mac running macOS 11 or newer, and your HyLight button
 - A Dexcom CGM with Dexcom Share turned on (Dexcom app > Settings > Share,
   with at least one follower added)
 - The Dexcom login of the person WEARING the sensor. Do not use a
   follower's account - it will log in but show no readings.


SET UP (about 5 minutes)
------------------------
1. Unzip, then drag HyLight.app into your Applications folder.
   (Which zip? Apple menu > About This Mac. "Apple M1/M2/M3..." = Apple
   Silicon zip. "Intel" = Intel zip.)

2. Plug in the button.

3. Open HyLight. The first time, macOS will say it can't verify the app,
   because it isn't from the App Store yet. This is expected. To open it:
     macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer:  click Done, open System Settings >
        Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, click "Open Anyway"
        next to HyLight, then enter your Mac password.
     macOS 14 or older:  right-click (or Control-click) HyLight.app, choose
        Open, then click Open.
   Still stuck? Open the Terminal app and paste this one line, then try again:
        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/HyLight.app

4. Your browser opens HyLight's settings page. Sign in with the Dexcom login
   of the person wearing the sensor, pick your region, and click Connect.

5. macOS may ask for permission. Say yes to both:
     - "Input Monitoring": needed to talk to the button. If you're sent to
       System Settings, switch HyLight ON, then quit and reopen HyLight.
     - Keychain access: this is where your Dexcom login is stored safely.
       If it asks for a password, that is your Mac login password.
       Choose "Always Allow".

6. The status line at the top of the page should say
   "Button connected - showing ... BG ...". You're set.

TIP: If you close the settings page, double-click HyLight again (or choose
"Open HyLight Settings" from the HyLight menu at the top of the screen) to
bring it back. It lives at http://127.0.0.1:8765 if you'd like a bookmark.


USING THE BUTTON
----------------
 Colors   GREEN  = in range (only shown when you tap the button)
          WHITE  = glucose is ramping up
          YELLOW = high: above your yellow threshold
          RED    = very high: above your red threshold
          PURPLE = low: below 70
          BLUE   = your reminder timer finished
 Tap          Shows your current status for 3 seconds, any time of day.
 Press & hold Mutes the current alert for a while. Hold again to un-mute.
 Double tap   Starts a reminder timer (default 20 minutes). The button
              flashes blue to confirm, then glows blue when time is up.

Automatic lights only happen inside your "active window" (default 11:30 AM -
8:00 PM). Change the window, thresholds, mute length and reminder time on
the settings page.


QUITTING AND RESTARTING
-----------------------
Quit any normal way: "Quit HyLight" at the bottom of the settings page, the
HyLight menu at the top of the screen, Cmd+Q, or right-click the Dock icon >
Quit. Each turns the light off. The goodbye page after "Quit HyLight" has a
"Restart HyLight instead" button if you meant to restart it.
If the app is ever force-quit, the button can stay lit. Just open HyLight
again and quit it normally.
To stop using HyLight: click "Disconnect Dexcom account" on the settings
page, quit, then delete the app.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
 "Button not detected": unplug and replug the button, and check Input
    Monitoring in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
 No light when you expect one: look at the status line at the top of the
    page. It shows the BG HyLight sees and what it is showing. Remember
    automatic lights only appear inside the active window.
 No BG number at all: Share is probably off, or you signed in with a
    follower's account. Disconnect, then sign in again with the wearer's.


FEEDBACK
--------
Please tell me about anything confusing, wrong, or annoying - including
whether the colors and timing feel right in real life. If something breaks,
send me this log file:  ~/.dexcom_led_button/hylight.log
(In Finder: Go menu > Go to Folder..., paste ~/.dexcom_led_button, press
Return.) It records your glucose readings and Dexcom username, but never
your password. Share it only if you are comfortable doing so.

Privacy: HyLight only talks to Dexcom's servers and to your own Mac. Your
Dexcom login is kept in your Mac's Keychain.
